ChromeOS Flex Guide

Transform your old PC or Mac into a fast, secure device for students.

  1. Get a USB flash drive (8GB or more).
  2. Install the Utility: Download the Chromebook Recovery Utility extension in Google Chrome.
  3. Create Media: Launch the utility and follow steps to create the installation media.
  4. Boot Device: Plug the USB into your old device and boot from the USB drive.
  5. Install: Follow the on-screen prompts to install ChromeOS Flex!

Impact:

Doing this extends the device lifespan by up to 5 years and prevents immediate e-waste.

Backup Device Setup Ideas

1. Smart Home Hub

Install apps like Google Home or Apple HomeKit. Mount it on a wall or leave it permanently plugged in the living room as a family controller.

2. Dedicated Dash Cam

Use an app like AutoBoy Dash Cam. Mount it in your car to loop-record your drives automatically. No internet needed!

3. Offline GPS & Media Player

Download Google Maps for offline use and load it up with Spotify playlists for road trips without draining your main phone's battery.
